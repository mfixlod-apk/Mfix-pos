# MFIX POS Development Roadmap

## Target
Transform MFIX POS from a basic point-of-sale application into a configurable retail POS system.

## Phase 1 - Application Control Center
- Central Settings screen
- Business profile
- POS behavior settings
- Receipt and printing preferences
- Backup/import/export controls

## Phase 2 - Printer Manager
- Add/remove printers
- Bluetooth, USB and network printer profiles
- Default printer selection
- 58mm/80mm paper width
- Test print
- Cash drawer trigger
- Receipt copies and automatic printing

## Phase 3 - Inventory Manager
- Products and categories
- Barcode and SKU
- Cost, sale and promotion prices
- Stock quantities and minimum stock alerts
- Suppliers
- Serial number / IMEI support
- Stock movement history
- Import/export CSV

## Phase 4 - POS Operations
- Cart editing
- Quantity and discount controls
- Multiple payment methods
- Split payments
- Returns and cancellations
- Opening/closing cash drawer sessions

## Phase 5 - Users and Reports
- Employee accounts
- Role permissions
- Daily sales and profit reports
- Inventory alerts
- Audit log

## Phase 6 - Integrations and Backup
- Yesh Invoice integration
- Local backup and restore
- Cloud sync option
